## Looks bad but isn't

- **"Fund this sleeve" refuses with "The most this book can fund right now is
  N%".** That is correct and deliberate. A sleeve that can only be partly funded
  would leave a position at a size nobody chose, so it abstains and tells you the
  sleeve that *would* work. Lower the slider to N% and it proceeds.
- **A card says "already at the sleeve target — nothing to buy."** Also correct.
- **`smoke-p0.ps1` reports SKIP for "fund execution not run".** Expected: the
  script is read-only unless you pass `-Execute`.
- **Section 3 has nothing to look at.** Expected when every backtest is healthy.
- **COW still counted in NAV.** Deliberate — see check 5.
